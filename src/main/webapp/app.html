Help: How to Use the System

  1. Register Appointment: fill in all fields marked *, then click Register. Note the appointment number shown.
  2. Display Appointment: enter the appointment number and click Search to see patient and appointment details.
  3. Calculate & Print Bill: enter the appointment number, click Calculate Bill, then Print Receipt.
  4. Reports: view a list of all appointments.
  5. Exit / Logout: always log out when you finish to keep patient data secure.
